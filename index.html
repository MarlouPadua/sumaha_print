Good to know

Your data stays on your phone. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only time a file leaves your phone is when you share or export it, or when you connect your own analysis server.
Prices are estimates. The ready-made presets reflect the Prompter's own printer (Epson EcoTank L5290, Epson 003 ink). Change every preset to your own printer, ink, paper and prices before you rely on a result.
