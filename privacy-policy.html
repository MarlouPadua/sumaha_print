Privacy Policy

Sumaha Print · by the Prompter, Marlou Padua

1. Who we are

Sumaha Print (the "App") is a print-cost calculator for print shops and their customers. It was made by Marlou Padua (the "Prompter", "we", "us"), who prompted an AI to build it. This policy explains what the App does with information. You can contact the Prompter at marloupadua555678@gmail.com.

In short: the App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The information you enter stays on your phone. We do not receive it, and we have no server that stores it. The only time your files leave your phone is when you choose to share or export something, or when you choose to connect your own analysis server.

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

2. Information the App keeps on your device

Everything below is saved in the App's private storage on your phone (a local database). It is created by you, and used only to make the App work.

  • Store details (Shop mode): store name, attendant, address, phone, email, business/tax ID, receipt footer, and your currency. Only what you type.
  • Presets and prices: paper, ink, printer and quality presets, setups, page-count tiers, price rounding rules, fees, and your Simple/Advanced settings view.
  • Calculation history: for each calculation, the file names, page counts, per-page ink coverage figures, the settings used, the price, a reference number, and the customer name if you typed one.
  • Sales: sale numbers, customer name, contact, notes, items, discounts, charges, payment types, payments and due dates, only if you use the Sales feature.
  • Stock and logs: ink levels, paper stock, timed prints and their change history.
  • Security keys: a signing key used to sign customer price files. It stays on the seller's phone (see section 5).
  • App state: which mode you use (Shop or Customer), whether you finished the first-run tour, and your last screen choices.
  • Customer mode: the shop's price file that you opened, and an optional name you type for your invoice.

Your documents: when you choose a PDF, image or Word file, the App reads it on your phone to count pages and measure ink coverage. It does not keep a copy of your document. The file name and page count are saved in the history record. Previews and analysis happen on your phone unless you turn on the optional analysis server (section 3).

3. When information leaves your device

The App does not send information to the Prompter. Information leaves your phone only in these cases, and each one starts with an action from you:

  1. Analysis server (optional, Advanced settings). If you type a server address in Settings, the App uploads the file you are analyzing to that address (and an API key, if you entered one) so the server can measure ink coverage. That server belongs to you or your shop. The Prompter does not run or receive anything from it. If you leave the address blank, analysis stays on your phone. Only connect a server you trust, and use an encrypted (https) address if the files are sensitive.
  2. Sharing and exporting. Receipts and invoices (PDF or image), backups and customer price files are created on your phone. If you share them, the file goes to the app or person you choose through the Android share sheet or the location you save to. From that moment their privacy rules apply, not ours.
  3. Printing. When you print, the document is handed to Android's print service and the printer or print app you choose.
  4. Links. Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, User Manual, the Prompter's social pages and support pages open in your browser or another app. Those sites have their own policies. The App itself is not linked to your accounts there.

4. What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of how you use the App.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information, because we do not have it.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or messages. The App opens files through Android's file picker, so it only sees the files you choose. The internet is used only for the features in section 3.

5. Backups, customer files and security

  • Backups (.sprint) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. If you set a password, the key comes from your password (PBKDF2), which is much stronger. If you do not set one, the file is protected only by a key built into the App: it stops casual reading and makes the file open only in Sumaha Print, but it is not strong protection. Use a password for any backup that holds customer or sales information.
  • A backup can include your history, sales, presets, store info, settings and your signing key. Whoever has the file (and the password, if any) can restore all of it. Keep backups safe.
  • Customer price files (.sprice) contain the prices, presets and store details you chose to include, and are encrypted. They are digitally signed (Ed25519) so the Customer mode can check that the shop made the file and that nobody changed it. They never include your sales or history.
  • Your information is protected mainly by your phone's own security. Use a screen lock, and keep your phone and backups safe. If you lose a phone, anyone who can unlock it can open the App.

6. Shops and their customers' information

A shop that records a customer's name, contact or payments in the App is the one responsible for that information. The Prompter cannot see it. If you run a shop in the Philippines, you are expected to handle personal information as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) requires, for example by collecting only what you need, keeping it safe and deleting it when no longer needed. Customer Mode does not send anything to the shop automatically. A customer decides what to share by sending an invoice or file themselves.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual presets, history records, sales and logs inside the App, and "Delete all presets" in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes its local data. Files you exported, shared or saved elsewhere (backups, receipts, price files) are not removed. Delete them yourself.
  • Because we hold none of your data, we cannot recover it for you. Make backups.

8. Who the App is for

Sumaha Print is a business tool for print shops, and a price checker for their customers. It does not ask for, or need, any personal details to work.

9. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a new feature that sends information somewhere, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Please check it from time to time (Settings → Help & about → Privacy Policy).
