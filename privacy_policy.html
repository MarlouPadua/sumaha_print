In short: the App works on your phone. We do not run any servers for it, we do not have accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not collect, receive or sell your data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, unless you choose to share or export it, or to use your own optional analysis server.

1. Information the App handles

All of the following stays on your device, in the App’s private storage:

  • Documents you select (PDF, images, and Word files when an analysis server is used). The App reads the pages to measure how much ink they need. A temporary copy may be created by the system file picker in the App’s cache.
  • Calculation results: page coverage, ink estimates, prices, copies and options. These are saved in History.
  • Presets and settings: paper, ink, printer, quality presets, setups, page-count tiers, currency, and the last state of the main screen.
  • Store information you type: store name, attendant, address, contact number, email or social page, tax/business ID, and receipt footer message.
  • Customer names you optionally type for a job.
  • Receipts (PDF or image) generated from the above.

2. How information is used

Only to provide the features of the App: calculating prices, showing History, restoring where you left off, and creating receipts. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local database in the App’s private storage on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere by the App. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please protect your device (screen lock, device encryption). Uninstalling the App deletes its stored data. Files you export or save (backups, receipts) are saved where you choose and are not protected by the App.

4. Optional analysis server

By default the App measures pages on your phone. If you enter a server address in Settings → Analysis server, the document you choose to calculate (and the optional API key you typed) is sent to that server, which you or someone you trust operates. We do not operate or have access to it. Please use a server you control, preferably over HTTPS. Leave the address blank to keep everything on-device.

5. Sharing

The App shares data only when you tap an action such as Receipt PDF / Receipt Image → Share, Save to phone, or Export. The file then goes to the app or folder you select. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Some screens contain links (for example the Prompter’s social pages, this policy, the Terms and the User Manual). Opening a link takes you to a website or app we do not control.

6. Permissions

  • File access: through the system file picker, only for files you pick or locations you choose to save to.
  • Internet: used only for the optional analysis server and for opening links.
  • The App does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone number.

7. Third-party components

The App is built with open-source libraries (for example Flutter, sqflite, file_picker, pdfx, pdf/printing, image, share_plus and url_launcher). They run inside the App on your device. The App does not send your data to their developers.

8. Your customers’ information

If you type customer names or store details and keep them in History or on receipts, you are responsible for handling that information lawfully (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 or the privacy laws that apply to you). You can delete any History record, delete all presets, or uninstall the App at any time.

9. Your choices

  • Leave optional fields (customer, attendant, address and so on) blank, and untick them so they never appear on receipts.
  • Delete records in History (confirmed twice).
  • Export your data when you want a copy, and delete the file when you no longer need it.

10. Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
