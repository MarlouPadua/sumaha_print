1. Getting started

  1. Choose your mode. The first time you open the App it asks how you will use it: Shop Owner (you run a print shop) or Customer (you want to check a price). You can switch later: Shop to Customer in Settings, Customer to Shop with the storefront button at the top of the Customer screen. Nothing is deleted when you switch.
  2. Shop owners: pick ready-made presets (a working starting point) or start clean. Then change the presets to match your own printer, ink, paper and prices.
  3. Quick tour. A short tutorial opens the first time each mode starts. Replay it from Settings → Help & about → Tutorial (Shop) or the help button (Customer).
The ready-made presets reflect the Prompter's own printer (Epson EcoTank L5290, Epson 003 ink, Philippines). Change them to yours before trusting results.

2. Shop mode: the main screen

Files

Tap Browse and choose a PDF, images, or a Word file. Pages are counted automatically. Use Add to put more files in the same job, hold and drag in "Files in this job" to reorder, Change to replace, and X to remove. A Word file must be used on its own.

Presets

  • Quick: choose one saved setup (paper + ink + printer + quality). This is the fastest way.
  • Custom: choose the paper, ink, printer and quality separately. Switching modes keeps what you chose.

The summary under the choice shows the paper size, how much ink that size uses compared with A4, the ink charge rule, and your ink and paper stock. Amber notices warn about things like a page count outside the preset's tier, low stock, or a paper with no price.

Print options

  • Target pages: ALL, a range like 1-5, or a list like 1, 3, 7. Select lets you tick pages.
  • Copies: use +/- or type a number (minimum 1). Print sides: 1 side or 2 sides.
  • Customer: optional name for the receipt.
  • Print after analyzing: sends the file to the print dialog first, then deducts ink and paper and shows the result.
  • Fees & extras: finishing fee per page and a one-time setup fee.
  • Advanced: color mode (auto-detect, all black & white, all color), analysis source (automatic, server, on-device) and including startup/cleaning ink.

Tap Analyze & Calculate. The App measures the ink on each page, then shows the price.

3. Reading the result

  • Final price is the biggest number. Below it: Paper, Ink, Fees, Setup, and Rounding if it changed the price ("Rounded from ...").
  • If you change anything after calculating, the price is marked outdated: calculate again.
  • Print analysis details → How this price was worked out walks through the job in plain language, with your own numbers: pages, paper, ink (with an example page), the total, and rounding.
  • The page-by-page table shows C/M/Y/K coverage, paper, ink and price for every page.

How the price is made

PartHow it works
PaperSheets used × the higher of your real cost per sheet (pack cost ÷ sheets) and the paper's Base rate. With 2 sides, two pages share a sheet.
Ink (estimated)Coverage of each color on each page, turned into mL by the ink model, then into cost with your bottle price. Quality, paper absorption, the paper's ink use for its size and any ink limit also affect it.
Ink (charged)Fixed price per page: a set amount for B&W and for color. By consumption with a minimum: the higher of the estimate and your minimum for that page.
FeesFinishing per page, printer maintenance fee per page, and startup ink if included.
SetupA one-time fee for the job.
RoundingOptional, applied last (section 4).

4. Price rounding

Settings → Price rounding. Turn it on, choose the step (for example 1, 0.50, 5) and how to round: to the nearest, always up, always down, or up only past your own limit. "Try it" shows the effect on sample amounts. Rounding applies to calculations, receipts, sales totals and, if you include it, customer price files. Every result shows the price before rounding and the rounding amount.

5. Receipts, sales and history

  • Receipt PDF / Image: from a result. Receipts use the store details you ticked in Store info & currency. They are for your records and are not official tax receipts.
  • Create sale: turns a job into a sale where you can add items, discounts, charges, payments, credit and a due date, and void it. On the Sales screen the figures (totals, cost, profit) are hidden every time you open it; tap the eye to show them for that visit.
  • History: every calculation is saved with its reference number. Open one to view, share a receipt, or edit and recalculate. Export and import of everything is done from Settings (see section 9).

6. Ink and paper tracking

Optional. Turn on Ink tracking in a Printer preset (levels per color) and Stock tracking in a Paper preset (sheets left). Choose automatic (deducted when you calculate) or manual (you tap deduct). On a result you can Undo a deduction (it is given back) and Redo it if that was a mis-tap. Ink and stock logs keep a history, and you can undo or redo changes there too. Low-stock alerts show on the main screen.

7. Settings

  • Simple / Advanced view: Simple shows only what changes a price or identifies something. Advanced shows everything, including print time, tiers and the analysis server. Hidden settings keep their values.
  • Store info & currency: tick the fields to print on receipts; choose your currency.
  • Price rounding: see section 4.
  • Presets: Setups, Paper, Ink, Printer, Quality. Add the ready-made presets at any time (existing ones are not duplicated).
  • Print time (Advanced): estimated time of a print. Time real prints and recalibrate. It never changes a price.
  • Page-count tiers (Advanced): names like "<20 pages" and "20+ pages" you can give to presets and setups. Renaming a tier updates the presets that use it.
  • Analysis server (Advanced): optional address of a Ghostscript server for more exact coverage. Blank = on your phone. The file you analyze is uploaded to this server.
  • Switch to Customer mode, Tutorial, Assumptions & limits (with a worked example), About the Prompter, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, User Manual.

Unsaved changes

In the preset editor, Store info and Price rounding, anything you changed but did not save gets an amber outline with "Changed · was: ..." and an Undo button. The Save bar counts the changes and Next change scrolls to each one. If you leave with unsaved changes, the App asks to Keep editing, Discard or Save.

8. Presets in detail

Paper

  • Size: pick from the list (Short, Long, Legal, A4, A3, A5, B5, photo sizes and more) or type your own, such as 5x7 in.
  • Ink use vs A4 (%): how much ink a page of this size uses compared with A4 (A4 = 100). It fills in from the size's area (Long is about 114%, 14% more) and you can change it; "Use the default for this size" resets it.
  • Paper type: plain, bond, recycled, inkjet quality print, cardstock, sticker, matte or gloss photo paper. A type suggests an Absorption factor (an estimate you can change).
  • Pack cost, Sheets per pack, Base rate: the price per sheet is the higher of real cost and base rate. A blank or 0 base rate means no minimum (for example photo paper priced only by its real cost).

Ink

  • Ink model: Manufacturer Yield (from the published page yield; the ready-made default), Reference Coverage, Custom Calibration (from your own measured mL) or Direct mL per coverage.
  • Reference page size: the page the yields were measured on (Epson: A4).
  • Charge mode and minimums: fixed price per page, or by consumption with a minimum for B&W and for color.
  • Bottle price, bottle volume and yield per color; maintenance and startup ink; calibration notes (Advanced).

Printer, Quality, Setups

  • Printer: maintenance fee per page, print-time numbers, and optional ink tracking per color.
  • Quality: ink multiplier, color density multiplier and an optional ink limit.
  • Setup: remembers a paper + ink + printer + quality combination and an optional tier. If you rename a preset, setups follow it. If you delete one, you are told which setups use it.

9. Backup, import and customer files

Export backup

Settings → Export backup. Choose what to include: store info, price rounding, app preferences, tiers, analysis server, signing key, presets, history, sales and logs. Ticking a setup automatically ticks the paper, ink, printer and quality presets it needs (locked while the setup is ticked). Add a password for real protection. The file is a .sprint file.

Import backup

Settings → Import backup. Pick the file, enter the password if it has one, choose what to import, and decide how to treat presets with the same name (keep both, skip or replace). Setups that come with their parts follow any renamed parts.

Customer price file

Settings → Export customer price file. It is encrypted and signed (.sprice). Choose the presets and settings to include, which store details to show, validity (days), a message, finishing and setup fees, and what customers may do:

OptionEffect
Let customers choose CustomOn: Quick (your setups) or Custom (their own paper, printing, quality). Off: they only pick one of your setups.

Send the file to your customer (chat, email, file share). It never contains your sales or history.

10. Customer mode

  1. Tap the folder button and open the shop's price file. The App checks the shop's signature and the expiry date.
  2. Choose your files, and optionally the pages.
  3. Choose an option: Quick (the shop's setups) and, if the shop allows it, Custom.
  4. Set copies and sides, optionally type your name for the invoice, then tap Calculate my price.
  5. The estimated total shows paper, printing, fees, setup and any rounding the shop applies. Open How this price is calculated for the steps. Save or share an invoice as a PDF or image.

Your files are analyzed on your phone, and nothing is sent to the shop by the App. The price is an estimate; the shop's own count and final price prevail. Run a shop? Tap the storefront button to switch to Shop mode.

11. Tips and troubleshooting

  • Prices look wrong: check every preset (paper pack cost and base rate, ink prices and minimums, quality multiplier). Open the result's "How this price was worked out".
  • "Paper has no price" warning: pack cost and base rate are both 0. Enter your paper cost.
  • Result says outdated: you changed something; calculate again.
  • Cannot see a setting: you may be in Simple view. Switch to Advanced in Settings, or tap "Show all" in a preset.
  • Word files: page count may be unknown and a Word file must be used alone.
  • Customer file will not open: it may have expired, been edited, or need a password. Ask the shop for a new one.
  • New phone: export a backup with a password, move it, then import it.
